## Issue templates

| template | purpose |
|---|---|
| bug_report.md | general breakage, asks for detect_env --json |
| sync_rate.md | the most common problem - sync rate below 80% |
| tts.md | hardware detection, voice mismatch, loudness |

`sync_rate.md` front-loads the two answers that resolve 90% of cases:
run gate1 first, and add a breathing overlay for subtle-motion scenes.
It also inlines the exact overlay snippet so people do not have to
hunt through PITFALLS.

`config.yml` disables blank issues and points to PITFALLS, GETTING-STARTED
and Discussions before anyone opens a new one.

`pull_request_template.md` asks for measured before/after numbers,
because this project's whole value proposition is that claims are measured.
